# Code of conduct

## Our standard

OpsCustodian contributors and maintainers must make participation respectful,
professional, and free from harassment. Constructive technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks, intimidation, discrimination, sexualized content,
doxxing, threats, and deliberate disruption are not.

Examples of positive participation include:

- focusing criticism on code, evidence, interfaces, and outcomes;
- respecting different experience levels and communication styles;
- protecting private organization, repository, and credential information;
- accepting maintainer decisions and helping document unresolved tradeoffs.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary concerns, contact the maintainers without including private or
sensitive details in a public issue. If no private channel is already available,
open a minimal public issue requesting private contact and omit the incident
details. Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban participation when necessary. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where practical, and
avoid exposing reporters or affected people.
